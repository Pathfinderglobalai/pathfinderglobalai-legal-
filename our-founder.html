Our Founder

The Story Behind PathFinderGlobal AI

Founder & CEO — De'Ann

There was a season in my life when I found myself standing between jobs, uncertainty, and a dream I had not yet learned how to name. My last customer service probationary job did not keep me, the short-term security position ended, and the elderly healthcare work I had taken on also came to an end. I was waiting for a good full-time position to call, but while I waited, something began to grow inside of me. Late at night, sitting in my second-story studio apartment, I would look out the window at the sky and the stars and think about my future. During the daytime, I would sit by that same window and watch the birds moving through the trees. In those quiet moments, one question kept coming back to me: "I wonder if a company would let me take about 13 minutes to walk around their busy company so I could decide whether I would want to work there—and perhaps even retire in that type of business?"

That question became more than a thought. Sometimes I took the bus, and sometimes I rented a car, and I visited several companies myself. I would ask, "Can I take 13 minutes of your time to take a walk through and feel out if a business like this would be a great fit for me?" Some managers told me that only Human Resources could decide whether I was a good fit and that I would have to go through the regular hiring process. Others told me that companies might think I was crazy for asking. But I was not trying to be difficult—I was trying to understand something that traditional job applications could not show me. I wanted to experience the environment, imagine myself in the role, and ask myself the most important question: "Can I see myself here?"

Then my brother, Simeon, gave me one of his old laptops that he had refurbished himself. On that computer, he had installed programs such as GIMP and Blender. I jokingly thought GIMP looked like some kind of cute beaver-looking hamster, but Blender—"Blender," as I kept thinking of it—really intrigued me. That old refurbished laptop became more than a computer to me. It became a doorway. Since I was not working at the time, I decided that instead of simply waiting for someone to give me an opportunity, I would try to create one. I began developing the idea for a mobile career app that could allow a person to see themselves in a career before committing to it—to experience the possibility of becoming a healthcare professional, an officer, a technology professional, a business leader, an aviation professional, a skilled trades-person, or another career, and to imagine what their future could look like.

"What if people could experience their future before they choose it?"

There were seventeen-hour days when I did not stop, and there were nights I stayed awake long past four o'clock in the morning, teaching myself how to design, studying everything I could find, and pushing the development of my career mobile app forward one step at a time. It was not always easy, but every late night and every long day was building toward something I could feel was worth it.

That is where PathFinderGlobal AI began—not from a huge office, a large investment, or a team of developers, but from a woman sitting by a window, looking at the stars at night and birds in the trees during the day, asking that simple but powerful question. I, CEO De'Ann, did not allow temporary job endings to become the ending of my story. I turned rejection into reflection, uncertainty into imagination, and an old refurbished laptop into the beginning of a global vision. PathFinderGlobal AI was born from my desire to help people make career decisions with greater confidence by allowing them to see, explore, and imagine themselves in the world of work before they commit. What began as my personal search for the right place to belong became a vision to help people everywhere discover where they might belong—and perhaps see a future they never knew was possible.

I consider myself to be a "Little Lotus Flower," and I keep going by holding on to God, and this Little Lotus encouragement: "Just Apply Perseverance, Pursue, Aimlessly."

Sincerely,

De'Ann Collie
