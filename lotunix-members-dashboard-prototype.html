LOTUNIX Workflow Vault · 13 original draft prompts

Each prompt asks for explicit inputs, clear assumptions, practical outputs, and review points. These are original working drafts; uniqueness across all other systems cannot be guaranteed. Do not paste confidential customer data into an AI tool without authorization.

01 · VISION

Business Opportunity Architect

Act as a business strategy analyst. My skills are [SKILLS], available time is [HOURS/WEEK], budget is [BUDGET], audience is [AUDIENCE], and market is [MARKET]. Generate 5 realistic business opportunities. For each, state customer pain, offer, validation experiment costing no more than [TEST BUDGET], key risks, and a measurable 14-day success signal. Clearly label assumptions and ask for missing critical information before recommending one.

02 · DISCOVERY

Evidence-to-Opportunity Scanner

Analyze these customer observations or interview notes: [NOTES]. Separate direct evidence from inference. Cluster recurring problems by urgency, frequency, willingness to pay, and ease of reaching customers. Propose 3 interview questions that could disprove each hypothesis. Do not invent market statistics or quotes.

03 · POSITION

Differentiation Decision Engine

Given my offer [OFFER], ideal buyer [BUYER], alternatives [ALTERNATIVES], and proof [PROOF], draft 3 positioning statements with a clear target, outcome, differentiator, and believable reason to trust. Identify any unsupported claim and suggest how to validate it.

04 · CREATION

Content-to-Asset Production Line

Turn this verified expertise [SOURCE MATERIAL] into a 7-day content plan for [CHANNEL]. Produce one flagship outline, three short posts, one email, and a repurposing map. Preserve factual accuracy, flag claims needing verification, and add one measurable call to action per asset.

05 · ATTENTION

Campaign Experiment Designer

My offer is [OFFER], audience [AUDIENCE], channel [CHANNEL], budget [BUDGET], and objective [OBJECTIVE]. Propose three ethical marketing experiments, with hypothesis, creative angle, tracking metric, stop condition, and review date. Avoid guaranteed results.

06 · CONNECTION

Customer Conversation Builder

Create a customer follow-up workflow for [CUSTOMER STAGE] and [PRODUCT]. Draft helpful, non-pushy messages for first contact, unanswered inquiry, and post-purchase check-in. Include personalization placeholders, consent checks, and an escalation path to a human.

07 · CONVERSION

Checkout Friction Auditor

Review this checkout copy and process [CHECKOUT DETAILS]. Identify possible confusion about product inclusions, price, currency, refunds, delivery, security, and next steps. Rank issues by severity and propose specific copy changes. Do not invent payment guarantees.

08 · AUTOMATION

Human-in-the-Loop Workflow Architect

Map this repeated process [PROCESS] from trigger to completion. List inputs, outputs, owners, tools, approval gates, exception cases, data sensitivity, and rollback steps. Recommend which steps to automate versus keep human-reviewed. Output a test plan and audit log schema.

09 · MONETIZATION

Offer Economics Simulator

Using price [PRICE], variable cost [COST], expected refund rate [REFUNDS], acquisition cost [CAC], and monthly fixed costs [FIXED], calculate contribution margin and break-even sales. Show formulas, assumptions, and best/base/worst scenarios. Do not invent missing figures.

10 · MULTIPLICATION

Capacity-Aware Scale Planner

My current delivery process is [PROCESS], weekly capacity [CAPACITY], bottleneck [BOTTLENECK], and quality target [QUALITY]. Propose a 30-day scale plan with standard operating procedures, automation candidates, quality checks, and leading indicators. Flag failure modes.

11 · OPTIMIZATION

Metric-to-Action Diagnostic

Given these results [METRICS] and targets [TARGETS], identify the 3 largest gaps. For each, provide plausible causes, evidence needed, a low-risk test, decision threshold, and owner. Separate correlation from causation.

12 · EXPANSION

Market Entry Readiness Gate

Assess entry into [NEW MARKET] for [OFFER]. Build a readiness checklist covering customer demand, localization, payment acceptance, privacy, taxes, fulfillment, support, and legal review. Mark unknowns as unknown; do not provide definitive legal advice.

13 · LEGACY

Durable Business Systems Blueprint

Given my business model [MODEL], values [VALUES], key processes [PROCESSES], and risks [RISKS], design a continuity plan with documentation priorities, delegated responsibilities, customer trust safeguards, data backup, and quarterly review milestones.
