LOTUNIX S.I.G.N.A.L. Workflow Vault · 13 signature prompts

S.I.G.N.A.L. = Situation, Inputs, Goal, Next actions, Assurance, Learning. Each original LOTUNIX workflow produces a deliverable, safeguards, and a measurable scorecard. These are original working drafts; uniqueness across all other systems cannot be guaranteed. Do not paste confidential customer data into an AI tool without authorization.

01 · VISION

Business Opportunity Architect

LOTUNIX S.I.G.N.A.L. WORKFLOW — VISION: Business Model Compass ROLE: Act as an evidence-aware business operations partner. Help me make a practical decision, not a motivational speech. S — SITUATION: I am working on vision. My context is [BUSINESS CONTEXT]. Ask up to 4 essential clarifying questions if needed. I — INPUTS: Use skills [SKILLS], constraints [CONSTRAINTS], market [MARKET], and resources [RESOURCES]. Label every missing input as UNKNOWN; never invent data, quotes, legal facts, or results. G — GOAL: My specific business outcome is [MEASURABLE GOAL] by [DATE]. Define a baseline and a realistic success threshold. N — NEXT ACTIONS: compare three customer problems, design one 14-day evidence-gathering experiment per problem, and recommend a reversible first move. Deliver a prioritized action table with owner, effort, dependencies, and first 48-hour step. A — ASSURANCE: rank evidence strength, feasibility, and customer urgency. Include a failure-mode review, customer impact, data-privacy check, and human approval gates where relevant. Distinguish facts, assumptions, and recommendations. L — LEARNING: Track qualified interviews, sign-ups, and cost per validated insight. Give a 7-day measurement plan, a decision rule to continue/pivot/stop, and 3 reflection questions. OUTPUT FORMAT: (1) One-paragraph diagnosis; (2) evidence vs assumptions; (3) prioritized action table; (4) copy-ready deliverable; (5) risk and review checklist; (6) 7-day scorecard. Use clear, direct language. If essential inputs are missing, provide a fill-in template instead of pretending certainty.
